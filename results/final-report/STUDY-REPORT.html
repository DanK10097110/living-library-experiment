Living Library Study

ALFWorld with Qwen3.5-9B: 3,104 episodes. Conditions: C (no memory), D (its own past trajectories), E (managed rule library), E-minus (same rule writer, no management). E and E-minus each ran two practice replicates of 300 games. Evaluation: 134 games in unseen rooms (OOD, primary) and 70 games in seen rooms (ID), with the library frozen.

Takeaways

  • Any memory helps: on OOD, D is 9 points above C and both libraries about 18 points above C.
  • E matches E-minus with about 5x fewer rules, and its library can be read and checked.
  • Variance between replicates is mostly a recipe lottery: one bad heat or look recipe costs 5-8 evaluation games, the size of the whole E vs E-minus gap.
  • The libraries keep improving through all 300 practice games: on the same 80 games, both average about 58% after 100 games (below D's 61%), about 64% after 200, and 67-70% after 300. A third or two thirds of the practice would not have been enough to beat D.
  • Next improvement: let E rewrite a recipe once its evidence is mixed (for example neg/shown > 0.4), not only when it is clearly harmful.
