One task, two recorded replays.

Each tab keeps the task and scene fixed, then shows two recorded attempts. Start with guided checkpoints to see the important decisions, or switch to Every action for the complete replay.

  • Side by side: checkpoints align meaningful moments, which can have different action numbers. Every action uses the same action index; a finished replay holds its final frame.
  • One after another: all actions in the first replay play before the second starts. Use the two chapter buttons to jump between them.
  • Condition comparisons: separate C (no memory) and E (rule library) evaluations of the exact same task instance. Newly added cases have successful E recordings; their recorded C baselines may fail.
  • Reflect & retry: a chronological initial attempt and retry of one practice game. Reflection is added before the retry; the rule manual stays fixed. A new rule is written after the retry succeeds.

How the images were made

Recorded policy actions were replayed in the official ALFWorld / AI2-THOR engine. The oracle maps object names to physical IDs and uses teleport viewpoints and ground-truth forceAction interactions. Commands rejected by TextWorld are shown as invalid and leave the physical scene unchanged. If an oracle viewpoint is blocked, nearby official viewpoints are tried with normal collision checks. No new language-model inference or weight training is performed.

These selected examples illustrate behavior. They do not isolate a causal effect of a rule or establish an overall benchmark result. Full source IDs, validity checks and both engines' outcomes are retained.
