    About these replays

    These are new RGB captures from the official ALFWorld / AI2-THOR simulator, driven by saved policy actions from this repository. No new Qwen inference is used.

    • The three scenes are restored from the official ALFRED trajectory data. The same recorded commands are also executed in TextWorld to check their validity and final outcome.
    • TextWorld instance names are mapped to the physical object IDs. Its “move … to” command is expressed as the THOR oracle controller’s equivalent “put … in” command.
    • The oracle grounds high-level actions in the 3D scene. Navigation uses teleport viewpoints, including object-focused viewpoints for a following pickup. Interactions use ground-truth entity IDs and the controller’s forceAction API, including occluded objects. This is a replay visualization, not a new evaluation of an embodied vision policy.
    • Both TextWorld and 3D goal results are retained in the replay data. Recorded and regenerated observations may differ.
    • C and E are separate recorded runs of the same heat task. Their comparison illustrates behavior and does not isolate a causal effect of a single rule.

    Engine: ALFWorld 0.4.2 · AI2-THOR 2.1.0 · 800 × 600 RGB.
    Reference: Official ALFWorld repository.
